Additionally, it offers analytical techniques, including titration, gravimetric, and volumetric analysis, to support comprehensive research and analysis. Pharmacognosy Lab Pharmacognosy lab is fully equipped and provides students facilities for performing varied kind of practicals pertaining to study of natural products, including morphological and microscopic analysis, extraction, isolation, identification, and standardization of crude drugs and herbal formulations, using equipment like HPTLC, Clavenger apparatus, Soxhlet apparatus, chromatographic columns, etc.. Machine Room The machine room offers state-of-the-art pilot scale production facilities for pharmacy students, equipped with the latest technology ranging from pilot scale tablet punching machine and a multipurpose machine complete with all necessary auxiliary extensions to facilitate the full range of unit operations involved in tablet production. Additionally, the facility houses a probe sonicator for the formulation of nanocarriers, a lyophilizer for processing various dosage forms, and a Brookfield viscometer for the characterization of novel formulations, ensuring high-quality assessment and development. Step 2 - Apply After successfully creating your candidate profile for CUCET, you can proceed with choosing the Date for your examination. Complete your profile by logging into your CUCET account and choose an examination date as per your convenience and preference.
