Physiotherapy is a health care profession that deals with the treatment and management of numerous ailments of the human body involving various systems of it. The Department of Physiotherapy, Chandigarh University is a department offers a Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) and Masters of Physiotherapy degree for the students aspiring to become physiotherapists. Some of the common areas covered are: Musculoskeletal system like joint pain, carpal tunnel syndrome, arthritis, post-fracture stiffness, etc Neurological system like stroke, paralysis, Parkinson’s disease, hemiplegia, paraplegia, quadriplegia, etc Paediatrics conditions like cerebral palsy, spina bifida, torticollis, delayed developmental milestones Geriatrics conditions like falls, incontinence, incoordination, dizziness Sports injuries like sprain, strain, shin splint, footballer knee, bursitis Cardio respiratory problems like COPD, respiratory distress asthma Physiotherapists help affected people to recover through employing various physiotherapeutic techniques like exercise, manual therapy, education & advice, improving their health, wellbeing, and quality of life. This profession helps to encourage a healthy lifestyle and facilitate recovery, enabling people to stay at work while helping them remains independent for as long as possible. The physiotherapy department of Chandigarh University has a full-fledged outpatient Physiotherapy department, equipped with modern facilities. The clinic caters to numerous problems of the general public as well as fellow staff members to resolve their problems and increase their work output. This also gives students the chance to apply their knowledge of clinical science in problem solving and therapeutic interventions. What makes Department of Physiotherapy unique? Check Out These Common Questions and Answers Do BPT and MPT programs provide real-time patient exposure and advanced clinical training? The Department of Physiotherapy at Chandigarh University ensures early and continuous clinical immersion. Students begin supervised patient interaction from the first year through in-house Physiotherapy OPD, hospital tie-ups, rehabilitation centres, and sports academies. Exposure includes musculoskeletal, neurological, cardiopulmonary, geriatric, pediatric, ICU, and sports injury cases. MPT (Orthopedics & Sports) students undergo advanced case management, evidence-based protocols, biomechanical assessments, and performance rehabilitation training. How experienced is the faculty and what research opportunities are available? The department has highly experienced faculty with strong academic and clinical backgrounds, including international exposure and active research engagement. Faculty and postgraduates contribute to evidence-based physiotherapy research, publications, and interdisciplinary projects. International academic collaboration with institutions like University of Wollongong enhances curriculum benchmarking, global best practices, and internship pathways. What advanced facilities and labs support hands-on physiotherapy training? The department houses fully equipped Physiotherapy, Electrotherapy, Exercise Therapy, Biomechanics & Kinesiology, Anatomy, and Physiology labs. Advanced tools include motion analysis systems, EMG, force plates, electrotherapy units, rehabilitation treadmills, balance systems, and sports performance equipment. The in-house OPD and sports rehabilitation setup provide real-time assessment, manual therapy practice, and injury prevention training under expert supervision. What are the realistic placement opportunities and highest packages offered? Graduates secure positions in multi-specialty hospitals, sports academies, neuro-rehabilitation centres, academic institutions, and private practice. Students have been placed with reputed organisations such as Fortis Healthcare, Apollo Hospitals, and Max Healthcare. Alumni also work in professional sports leagues and several graduates have established successful physiotherapy clinics. How strong is the Sports Physiotherapy ecosystem and industry collaboration? The department maintains structured collaborations with leading sports and healthcare organisations including Minerva Academy and Motherhood Hospital. Students gain exposure through industrial visits (Gait Lab at PGI), expert talks, on-field injury management workshops, and sports-specific rehabilitation protocols. The state-of-the-art sports rehab labs train students in athlete assessment, performance enhancement, return-to-play planning, and injury prevention strategies.
