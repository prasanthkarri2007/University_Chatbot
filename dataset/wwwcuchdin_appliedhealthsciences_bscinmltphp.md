MLT at CU provide real laboratory training from the beginning? Yes. The B.Sc. MLT program ensures hands-on exposure from early semesters. Students train in Clinical Biochemistry, Hematology, Microbiology, Histopathology, Immunology, and Molecular Diagnostics labs equipped with modern diagnostic instruments. Practical sessions are integrated with theory to build clinical accuracy and technical competence. Are internships and hospital trainings genuinely supported? The program includes mandatory clinical internships and summer training in reputed hospitals and diagnostic centres such as Fortis Healthcare, Max Healthcare, Dayanand Medical College & Hospital, Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education and Research, and Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital and Research Centre. Students work on real diagnostic samples, QC protocols, and reporting systems. What roles can students expect after B.Sc. MLT? Graduates work as Medical Laboratory Technologists, Clinical Lab Executives, QC Officers, Molecular Diagnostic Technicians, Research Assistants, and Lab Supervisors in hospitals, pathology labs, pharma companies, and research centres such as Dr. Lal PathLabs, Thyrocare Technologies Ltd., Metropolis Healthcare Ltd., Apollo Diagnostics, and Sun Pharmaceutical. How strong is the faculty and infrastructure? The department is led by experienced faculty with strong clinical and research backgrounds. Advanced diagnostic labs simulate real hospital environments with automated analyzers, microscopes, centrifuges, PCR systems, and quality-controlled workflows to ensure precision-based learning. What makes CU’s MLT program different? The program emphasizes precision diagnostics, patient-centred laboratory services, case-based learning, molecular diagnostics exposure, stem cell awareness initiatives, research publications, and industry collaborations.
