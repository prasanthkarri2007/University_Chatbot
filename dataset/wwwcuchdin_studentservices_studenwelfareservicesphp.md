The Department of Student Welfare (DSW) is a student care and supervision centre that serves as a liaison between students and administration. DSW closely works with students across all years and all societies to explore ways to enhance the experience, from education to student life to career planning, and works with them to develop innovative solutions to issues and concerns. The Department of Student Welfare has professionally trained staff to help you through the unanticipated problems that sometimes come your way. The main purpose behind setting up such service centres is to contribute towards the development of an academic environment that promotes productive learning and academic success while abolishing the stressing and challenging situations that could arise for one or another reason. Chandigarh University maintains student welfare services that are impartially available to all University students. From academic to a social or psychological problem, students can avail of help for anything they face. The most distinguished feature of CU's Student Care Centre (SCC) is the availability of timely – within 24 hours and single-spot solutions. Students can approach SCC (located in each academic block) in case of any problem related the administration and student services. DSW provides a favorable environment to fulfill students' academic and extra-curricular needs and stand devoted to ensure their success. Student Centric Chandigarh University is student‐centric campus with more than 17000 students, various key functions are ably supported by committed student bodies. Regular feedback mechanism and transparent system makes the learning experience enriching. Open House is a unique program that promotes transparency and feedback. Open house sessions are held at the beginning of each semester to bring forth the common problems faced by students and suggestions for improvement of the learning process. Feedback is a vital part of communication that if acknowledged in affirmative way can improve the working of the organization. Feedback mechanism helps to identify the critical areas of concern. Chandigarh University highly appreciates and work-on the suggestions and feedback given by students. We encourage students to put forward their fair opinions that can make CU a better place. Our robust online (through the website or UIMS) and offline (through suggestion boxes placed in different departments) feedback mechanism can be easily accessed by students.
