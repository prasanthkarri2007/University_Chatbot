Chandigarh University has signed MOU with the most successful private university in Germany, the FHM University of Applied Science, Germany. This MOU is a move to broaden our international tie-ups and bring the best of the global world to our students. This MOU covers exchange programs for both students and faculty. Hochschule Wismar, the third-biggest university in Germany and Chandigarh stepped into collaboration on June 20, 2015. The objective of this Mutual Accord is to foster the exchange of information and materials of mutual interest and to promote the exchange of faculty, students and scholars as well. Friedrich Schiller University (FSU) and Chandigarh University (CU) are in collaboration since September 10, 2015. The purpose of this Memorandum is to sustain an effective Cooperation between CU and FSU in order to facilitate the students with the best practices of both the universities and to promote the exchange of staff, students and scholars. Berlin International University of Applied Sciences is a private, non-profit institution of higher education in Berlin, Germany. It was founded in 2012 as Campus of Bahçeşehir University Istanbul and promoted in 2014 as a university in its own right by the state of Berlin. The Leuphana University Lüneburg emerged from the special legal mandate of the Lower Saxony state parliament to rethink university and create a model university for the Bologna Process. It is supported by a foundation under public law and sees itself as a place for free thirst for knowledge, imagination, personal development and social commitment. For its studies, it has developed a multi-award-winning study model with three schools, which is still unique in Germany today. Four faculties are the sponsors of transdisciplinary science initiatives, the Leuphana's research priorities in the fields of education, culture, sustainability, and management and entrepreneurship. The Leuphana University Macromedia University of Applied Sciences is a multi-campus university privately sponsored by Macromedia GmbH, based in Stuttgart, with other locations in Munich, Freiburg, Frankfurt/Main, Cologne, Leipzig, Hamburg and Berlin. Due to the central location of Macromedia’s city campuses, we can offer our current 4,500 students a stimulating urban study environment that also permits easy access to students from surrounding regions.
