The Department of Physiotherapy at Chandigarh University ensures early and continuous clinical immersion. Students begin supervised patient interaction from the first year through in-house Physiotherapy OPD, hospital tie-ups, rehabilitation centres, and sports academies. Exposure includes musculoskeletal, neurological, cardiopulmonary, geriatric, pediatric, ICU, and sports injury cases. MPT (Orthopedics & Sports) students undergo advanced case management, evidence-based protocols, biomechanical assessments, and performance rehabilitation training. The department has highly experienced faculty with strong academic and clinical backgrounds, including international exposure and active research engagement. Faculty and postgraduates contribute to evidence-based physiotherapy research, publications, and interdisciplinary projects. International academic collaboration with institutions like University of Wollongong enhances curriculum benchmarking, global best practices, and internship pathways. The department houses fully equipped Physiotherapy, Electrotherapy, Exercise Therapy, Biomechanics & Kinesiology, Anatomy, and Physiology labs. Advanced tools include motion analysis systems, EMG, force plates, electrotherapy units, rehabilitation treadmills, balance systems, and sports performance equipment. The in-house OPD and sports rehabilitation setup provide real-time assessment, manual therapy practice, and injury prevention training under expert supervision. Graduates secure positions in multi-specialty hospitals, sports academies, neuro-rehabilitation centres, academic institutions, and private practice. Students have been placed with reputed organisations such as Fortis Healthcare, Apollo Hospitals, and Max Healthcare. Alumni also work in professional sports leagues and several graduates have established successful physiotherapy clinics. The department maintains structured collaborations with leading sports and healthcare organisations including Minerva Academy and Motherhood Hospital. Students gain exposure through industrial visits (Gait Lab at PGI), expert talks, on-field injury management workshops, and sports-specific rehabilitation protocols. The state-of-the-art sports rehab labs train students in athlete assessment, performance enhancement, return-to-play planning, and injury prevention strategies.
