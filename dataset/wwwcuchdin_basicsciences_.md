Analytical Chemistry Lab Analytical chemistry focuses on identifying and measuring chemical substances with accuracy. Hands-on training includes modern testing methods, data interpretation and quality analysis used in environmental, industrial and laboratory settings. Physical Chemistry Lab Physical chemistry connects chemical processes with physical laws. Practical work helps students understand energy changes, reaction behavior and molecular interactions that explain how chemical systems function. Instrumentation Lab This lab introduces students to advanced chemical instruments and measurement tools. Training emphasizes operation, calibration and data analysis to build technical proficiency required in professional laboratories. Inorganic Chemistry Lab Inorganic chemistry studies metals, minerals and coordination compounds through structured experiments. Students gain exposure to synthesis, characterization and applications relevant to materials and industrial chemistry. Advance Chemistry Lab The advanced chemistry lab integrates multiple chemical techniques in complex experiments. Students apply theoretical knowledge to real problems, strengthening analytical thinking and laboratory independence. General Chemistry Lab General chemistry provides foundational laboratory experience in chemical reactions and measurements. Students develop essential skills, safety practices and scientific understanding needed across all chemistry disciplines. Polymer Chemistry Lab Polymer chemistry focuses on the preparation and testing of polymeric materials. Practical learning covers material properties, processing techniques and applications used in manufacturing and product development. Mathematical Research Lab This lab strengthens logical reasoning and problem solving through mathematical exploration. Students work with proofs, models and analytical methods that support advanced study and interdisciplinary applications. Computing Lab The computing lab applies mathematical concepts using computational tools. Students practice programming, numerical methods and simulations to solve real world problems efficiently. Digital Systems & Applications Lab This lab introduces digital logic and system design through practical exercises. Students learn how digital components function and are applied in electronics, automation and communication systems. Modern Physics Lab Modern physics exposes students to experiments that explain atomic and quantum phenomena. Step 1 Signup Step 2 Apply for CUCET Step 3 Admission Step 1 - Signup Sign up or Register for CUCET with basic details (Name, Email ID, Mobile No., State, Gender and Password). Your Email ID will act as your Username for the portal while the password will be set by you upon registration. On successful registration, you will receive the confirmation message on your registered contact number and Email. Use the Username and Password to Login to your CUCET Account. Step 2 - Apply After successfully creating your candidate profile for CUCET, you can proceed with choosing the Date for your examination. Complete your profile by logging into your CUCET account and choose an examination date as per your convenience and preference. Step 3 - Submit You become eligible for admission into Chandigarh University after clearing the CUCET examination. Following your attempt at CUCET, the result will shortly be intimated, based on which you can submit your application for admission and avail merit-based scholarship.
