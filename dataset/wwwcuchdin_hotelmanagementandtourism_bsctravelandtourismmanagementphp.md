Check Out These Common Questions and Answers How does UITHM at Chandigarh University prepare students for global tourism and hospitality careers? The program provides global exposure through collaborations with international organisations like PATA, participation in global tourism forums, workshops with international experts, and learning aligned with international tourism trends and practices. What kind of hands-on industry exposure do students receive at UITHM, Chandigarh University? Students gain hands-on experience in the Tourism Lab through itinerary planning, ticketing systems, tour operations, role-play simulations, industry visits, internships, workshops, and real-world tourism projects. Which tourism and hospitality sectors can students work in after studying at UITHM, Chandigarh University? Graduates work across airlines, travel agencies, tour operations, destination management companies, tourism boards, adventure tourism, cruise and resort operations, and hospitality entrepreneurship. How strong is the placement support at UITHM, Chandigarh University? Students receive structured training in personality development, grooming, interview preparation, and recruitment readiness, leading to placements with organisations like Reliance Jio World Centre, Thomas Cook, VFS Global, EaseMyTrip, and Indigo.
