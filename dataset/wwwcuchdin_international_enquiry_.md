National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) is an autonomous institution of the University Grants Commission (UGC) that assures ‘quality’ as the defining element of Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) in India. NAAC functions through its council comprising of influential educational administrators, policy makers and senior academicians from a cross-section of the Indian higher education system. It conducts assessment and accreditation of Higher Educational Institutions such as colleges, universities or other recognised institutions in terms of their performance related to the educational process and outcomes, curriculum, faculty, teaching-learning process, research undertaken, governance, new initiatives, and student services. On fulfilling the set parameters, NAAC scores the HEI on a 4 point scale that further denotes grade, and places them amongst the elite and prestigious Higher Educational Institutions of India.
