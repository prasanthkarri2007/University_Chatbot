Approx 120 students of BE (CSE) were participated and students were very much engaged in the concepts explained by the expert. By tracing the key ideas of today's most popular systems to their origins in research, the class highlights key developments in operating system design over the last two decades and illustrates how insight has evolved to implementation. The session was very interesting and students were really enthusiastic to learn the concepts of advanced operating system.
