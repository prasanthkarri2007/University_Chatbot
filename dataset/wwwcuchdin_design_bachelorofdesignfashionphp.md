Check Out These Common Questions and Answers How does Chandigarh University balance creativity with technical garment construction in Fashion Design? Chandigarh University follows a practice-driven curriculum that combines creativity with technical training. Students gain hands-on experience in garment construction, pattern making, textile dyeing and printing, fashion illustration, and trend forecasting while developing innovative garments and accessories. What kind of fashion labs and studios are available for Fashion Design students at Chandigarh University? Students learn in specialised Garment Construction Labs, Pattern Making Labs, and Textile Dyeing & Printing Studios. These well-equipped facilities allow students to design, prototype, and produce garments through real studio-based learning. Which fashion design software and digital tools are taught during the Fashion Design program at Chandigarh University? The programme integrates digital fashion tools such as CLO 3D, Tukatech, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, and CorelDRAW to train students in digital design, fashion communication, and modern garment visualisation techniques. How does Chandigarh University provide industry exposure and placements in Fashion Design? Students receive strong industry exposure through workshops by designers, field visits, and collaboration with apparel companies. Graduates have secured placements with brands such as Tommy Hilfiger, Calvin Klein, Arvind Fashions, ZARA, Reliance Brands, and Vero Moda. Is CUCET mandatory for Fashion Design at Chandigarh University? CUCET is not compulsory for admission. However, students can take the test to get up to 100% scholarship.
