HR Lab This lab integrates human resource management with digital technologies using platforms like OrangeHRM, Zoho People, UiPath, and Power BI. Artificial Intelligence Lab Students explore modern AI tools and platforms such as ChatGPT, Google Colab, Hugging Face, Runway ML, and DataRobot to develop intelligent digital solutions. Digital Health Lab Students explore healthcare data systems and digital health technologies using tools like DHIS2, OpenMRS, Zoho Creator, and Microsoft Power BI. The lab focuses on health data management, survey collection, and visualization using platforms such as Epicollect5 and Tableau Public to support evidence-based public health insights. Fintech Lab This lab immerses students in the world of financial technology, blockchain, and decentralized finance. With tools like FINOS, Open Bank Project and MetaMask, learners gain exposure to API banking, digital ledgers and smart contracts while developing accounting, tax and payroll automation skills through advanced finance software. Finance Lab Students develop practical financial management and taxation skills using tools such as MS Excel 365, Zoho Books, Genius Software, and GST Simulation platforms. The lab enables learners to analyze financial data, manage compliance processes, and simulate stock market operations through NEST while creating insightful dashboards using Power BI and Tableau. Trading Simulation Lab Students gain real-time trading exposure using TradingView for stock and crypto markets. The lab develops analytical thinking through charting, technical analysis and market tracking, enabling learners to simulate trades, test strategies and understand global financial trends in a risk-free digital environment. Digital Marketing Lab This lab develops digital marketing capabilities through platforms such as WordPress, Canva, CapCut, Mailchimp, and ChatGPT. Students design websites, create digital content, and manage online campaigns while gaining practical exposure to SEO, branding strategies, and AI-assisted marketing workflows. Strategy Simulation Lab Powered by Harvard Publishing simulations, this lab helps students develop strategic thinking and managerial decision-making. Learners engage in interactive business challenges that enhance problem-solving, technical understanding and leadership skills, preparing them for complex corporate environments through experiential, scenario-based learning. Supply Chain Operations Lab This lab trains students in logistics, inventory and operations management using SCM Globe, Zoho Inventory and AnyLogic. Learners simulate supply chain processes, forecast demand and visualize data to optimize performance. It provides practical understanding of end-to-end supply chain dynamics and real-time decision-making. Design Thinking & Innovation Lab A creative space where students explore problem-solving and innovation using Lucidchart, Miro, Canva and more. The lab nurtures design thinking, branding and data visualization abilities, encouraging learners to merge analytical reasoning with creativity for developing business solutions and impactful presentations.
