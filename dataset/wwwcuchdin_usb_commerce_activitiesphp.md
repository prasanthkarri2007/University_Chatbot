The Department of Commerce organized an expert lecture on "IPR PATENTS & DESIGN PROCESS". The expert of the session was Dr. Nitish Kumar Sharma (Assistant Professor). This expert session was defined as the basic clarity of “What is Intellectual Property?" He discussed different aspects of Intellectual Properties, like what patent rights are, rights awareness, the process of design associated with them, etc. The expert lecture provided valuable awareness on the various aspects of IPR and gave an insight into awareness of rights in IPR and filing procedures. Students were taught how IPR restricts copying others’ ideas and how this will help the development of new ideas for the betterment of societyThey were also given a platform to discuss how small innovations influence societies at large and how they should indulge in innovating and patenting ideas that will be useful for all classes of individuals in society.
