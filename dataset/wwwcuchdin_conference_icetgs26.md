International Conference on Educational Technology and Global Sustainability (ICETGS-2026) | Chandigarh University Toggle navigation Home About ICETGS 2026 Chandigarh University UITTR Committee Advisory Organizing Schedule Conference Theme Guidelines Contact Us Registration ICETGS-2026 3 rd International Conference on Educational Technology and Global Sustainability 07 th -08 th April, 2026 Organized by: University Institute of Teachers Training and Research Chandigarh University, Mohali, Punjab Register About the Conference The 3rd International Conference on Educational Technology and Global Sustainability (ICETGS 2026) will be organized in a blended mode by the University Institute of Teachers Training and Research, Chandigarh University, on April 07th and 08th, 2026. Building on the success of its inaugural edition, this conference continues to serve as a platform for global thought leaders, academicians, innovators and policymakers to explore the evolving convergence of education, technology and sustainable development. The first and second editions of the conference led to the publication of special issues in Scopus-indexed journals, demonstrating the quality of research contributions and the interdisciplinary nature of the scholarly work presented. ICETGS 2026 will once again provide a vibrant forum to delve into cutting-edge trends in educational technology, leadership and management, while advocating for equity and inclusivity, fostering sustainable development and addressing the needs of underrepresented communities in a global context. As we advance further into the digital age, ICETGS 2026 invites you to join this dynamic intellectual community, share your insights and collaborate on innovative solutions. Together, we can continue to leverage education and technology to create a more inclusive, sustainable and interconnected world. About Chandigarh University Chandigarh University stands as a prominent institution within the Indian educational landscape, distinguished by its integration of professional and academic excellence. Notably, the institution has garnered significant recognition with the esteemed NAAC A+ Grade, placing it in the upper echelons of the top 5% of universities in India. Additionally, it commands a position of prominence ranking within the top 1.7% of institutions across Asia, as per the prestigious QS World Rankings for the Asian region. Chandigarh University claims an expansive and intelligently designed campus across the 150 acres with world-class infrastructure and facilities, thus contributing to an environment conducive to scholastic and personal growth. Remarkably, Chandigarh University serves as a global microcosm, attracting learners from all corners of India, representing 28 states and 8 Union Territories as well as welcoming scholars from 54 nations worldwide. This multicultural tapestry fosters an environment characterized by diversity of ethnicities and the convergence of cherished traditions from across the globe. Chandigarh University's commitment to internationalization is further evidenced by its collaboration with more than 460+ top-ranked universities in 90 countries.
