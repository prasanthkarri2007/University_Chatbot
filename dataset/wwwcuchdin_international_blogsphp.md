The opening of Taiwan Education Centre at Chandigarh University has brought innumerable opportunities for students and researchers alike. The centre offers a variety of programs ranging from online semester exchange programs to lab internships and visiting research programs. In the age of globalization, the world is becoming more interconnected, and educational institutions are looking for ways to enhance their global reach. Chandigarh University is one such institution that has been welcoming inbound mobility from various countries, including Azerbaijan. Chandigarh University has always been at the forefront of promoting global education and facilitating cross-cultural exchange. The University has established partnerships with several renowned institutions across the globe, providing students with opportunities to study in different parts of the world and gain a unique perspective on diverse cultures and academic systems. One such partnership that has been making waves is the collaboration between Chandigarh University and the University of North Alabama (UNA). Chandigarh University received 106 Exchange Students for Aug/Sept 2022 Intake from countries like France, Germany, Russia, Mexico, Philippines, Bangladesh, Kyrgystan, Nigeria and Sudan. Congratulations to the students who transferred to the University of North Alabama from Chandigarh University for successfully completing their degrees! It's been quite a learning journey for these students, and they deserve recognition for their hard work and dedication. In an exciting development for the students of Chandigarh University, Disney is back and ready to recruit for its cultural immersion and internship experience program. After a long hiatus of three years due to the pandemic and lockdowns, the entertainment giant is finally resuming its recruitment efforts. This has brought back the unique opportunity for the students of Chandigarh University to gain valuable work experience and cultural exposure while working at one of the most well-known entertainment companies in the world. Chandigarh University's Office of International Relations (OIR) announces the onboard of its first cohort of Global Internship Students for 2022-23. This cohort will be interning at the 5-star Malaysian property, Pullman Hotel Kuala Lumpur. The internship will span over a semester-long duration and will be paid with a stipend of 600 Ringgit/month, along with free accommodation and duty meals. This is the 13th batch of students from Chandigarh University who will be participating in the international internship program.
