Reaffirming its status as one of the country’s premier higher educational institutions, Chandigarh University once again makes headlines with outstanding placement results. The renowned American IT company, EPAM has selected 70 Chandigarh University students from the CSE IT and MCA disciplines, making it the highest number of campus selections by EPAM from any single higher educational institution in India. Initiating their professional journey with EPAM, these students have been selected for the role of Junior Software Test Automation Engineers. These placements demonstrated CU’s commitment to research-driven education and global excellence and the institute’s standing in the region. Overall, Chandigarh University hosted over 1100 companies for placements and generated 9000+ placement offers with highest international placement package at ₹1.7 Cr and national placement package ₹54.70 LPA.
