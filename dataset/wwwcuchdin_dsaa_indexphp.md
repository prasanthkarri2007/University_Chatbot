Empowering students through comprehensive welfare services, residential excellence, and administrative support at Chandigarh University. “At Chandigarh University, the Department of Student Affairs & Administration (DSA&A) is committed to a vibrant, safe, and inclusive campus that enables academic excellence and character. World-class residential services, responsive student support, and a rich co-/extra-curricular ecosystem are benchmarked to the standards of top global universities. Leadership development and community engagement build service-driven leadership, resilience, and empathy. Our systems are agile, accountable, and student-first—empowering every learner to become a capable, confident, and compassionate global citizen.” The Department of Student Affairs at Chandigarh University nurtures a supportive, inclusive, and vibrant student ecosystem. From welfare initiatives and mental health support to leadership training and cultural events, DSAA ensures holistic growth of every student — academically, socially, and personally.
