ABET, the Accreditation Board for Engineering and Technology, is a globally recognized organization that accredits college and university programs in applied and natural science, computing, engineering, and engineering technology. ABET accreditation ensures that educational programs meet specific quality standards set by industry professionals, ensuring that graduates are well-prepared for their careers. ABET accreditation holds immense importance for students for multifarious reasons. Primarily, it guarantees that the program a student is enrolling in adheres to rigorous academic standards, ensuring a high-quality education. Furthermore, ABET accreditation provides global recognition, making graduates more marketable in the professional job market both nationally and internationally. Ultimately, it ensures that the students’ degrees will be recognized by employers and professional licensing bodies, facilitating their professional growth and mobility. With the recent ABET accreditation, CU's Engineering and Computer Science programs have ascended to new heights of excellence. This accreditation is a testament to the university's dedication to maintaining high academic standards, investing in state-of-the-art infrastructure, and fostering a vibrant learning environment. The rigorous evaluation process conducted by ABET assesses various aspects, including curriculum, faculty qualifications, research opportunities, facilities, and student outcomes.
