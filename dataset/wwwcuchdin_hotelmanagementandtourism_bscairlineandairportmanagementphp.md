Check Out These Common Questions and Answers How does Chandigarh University provide hands-on aviation training for Airlines and Airport Management students? Chandigarh University offers immersive training through a fully equipped Aviation Lab featuring a mock aircraft cabin with aircraft seats, overhead lockers, PSU panels, safety kits, food carts, and PA systems. Students also train in Grooming, F&B, Housekeeping labs and gain exposure through airport and hotel industry visits. Which airport and airline operations are covered during training at Chandigarh University? Students learn passenger handling, ground handling services, aviation safety procedures, cabin services, airport logistics, and computerized reservation systems using Amadeus and Galileo. Practical exposure is enhanced through industrial training and real airport visits. What international exposure opportunities are available for Airlines and Airport Management students at Chandigarh University? Students receive global exposure through exchange programs with Massey University (New Zealand), Sainte-Marie de Chavagnes (France), and IMI Switzerland. They also gain international internship opportunities such as the Walt Disney Internship Program. How do industry tie-ups strengthen Airlines and Airport Management training at Chandigarh University? Collaborations with FWSTC and aviation partners provide certifications, internships, workshops, and guest lectures. Graduates secure placements with top airlines and organisations including Air India, Emirates, Qatar Airways, Vistara, Singapore Airlines, British Airways, Delhi Duty Free, and BWFS. Is CUCET compulsory for Airlines and Airport Management at Chandigarh University? CUCET is not compulsory for admission. However, students can take the test to get up to 100% scholarship.
