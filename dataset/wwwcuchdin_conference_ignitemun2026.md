Through its carefully selected committees and an engaging delegate experience, IGNITE MUN will develop new approaches/cross-disciplinary practices for innovative thinking and accountability/reachable solutions within the context of international relations (IR). In its first year, also known as "IGNITE MUN 2026," it will provide the foundation for a culture of high-quality diplomacy, debate, and young leader development that has an emphasis on research, negotiation, collaboration, and public speaking. Additionally, the IGNITE MUN event is designed to give young people a voice and raise their level of awareness about IR, policy-making, and global development. The goal of this conference is to help delegates to think critically, act ethically, and lead with confidence. About the Conference The member schools of UITTR have the honor to invite you to our inaugural Model UN conference, IGNITE MUN, which will take place April 1 - 2, 2026. The purpose of this event is to provide a chance for diplomacy, conversation, and international cooperation among the youth. The goal of IGNITE MUN 2026 is to set a standard of excellence in the Model UN community in terms of providing an academically rigorous and intellectually stimulating environment for participants. By providing the first edition of this event, UITTR hopes to create an ongoing tradition of promoting critical thinking, developing leadership skills, and raising students’ awareness of global issues. Model UN conferences give youth a unique opportunity to participate in discussions about current issues facing the world today, to learn how to do research as well as speak in public with confidence and to develop their negotiation and consensus-building skills. At this year's conference, we expect to see many excited delegates participating from different institutions across the country. There will also be a diverse group of experienced conference leaders as part of the Executive Board of the conference. All committees across the various topics will have stimulating discussion agendas that all delegates will work toward developing and creating an inclusive environment for even more diverse participants by combining high school and collegiate attendees into one epic collaborative setting. Lastly, beyond the formal committee sessions, there will be ample opportunities for networking, cross-cultural exchanges, and networking opportunities that will provide delegates with new perspectives and much-needed motivation to become leaders in a global environment. At this time we are excited about the global debate and collaboration to create a positive lasting impact to provide the possibility of encouraging a more conscientious, diverse, inclusive and diplomatic way of conducting the future of society through developing and utilizing innovative solutions, providing leaders with actionable tools & building productive collaborative organizations to leverage their energy and ability in order to maximize their overall impact on how they will be remembered and their legacies will be recorded. We are looking forward to hosting all of our members at the two-day IGNITE Model United Nations in 2026. Knowledge Participating in MUN is a great way to gain valuable experience as a student. Through deep discussions about global politics, the international system and current global problems, delegates will be able to analyze a country’s history and foreign policy and envision how it will behave in the future. Every aspect of a country’s history is debated in depth and investigated through critical thought, analytical reasoning and communication using diplomacy. Discipline By adhering to formal rules of order and procedure as well as a defined procedure for MUN’s structure, delegates will learn not only how to demonstrate professionalism, appropriate use of time , and how to respect the opinions of those who hold different perspectives. The conference ambiance supports the values of equality and fairness through collaborative efforts; ultimately leading to an individual with increased levels of self-confidence and a sense of responsibility as an individual. Socializing A major part of developing long-term friendships and establishing networks of like-minded individuals is through participation in IGNITE MUN. In addition to forming alliances with one another and working together to draft resolutions, delegates will also have the opportunity to socialize and share cultural differences, develop the means to share their ideas and opinions and develop the ability to relate with others. Soft Skill Development The conference promotes the development of necessary skills for future academic success and for personal/business success after high school through the development of public speaking, articulation skills, negotiation skills, leadership skills, and teamwork skills. This will develop a delegate's confidence in expressing their ideas and supporting their position, persuading and convincing other people through personal integrity. Thought-Provoking Committees with engaging agendas. Certificates provided to all delegates. Networking opportunities with students from diverse institutions.
