Check Out These Common Questions and Answers How does Chandigarh University train students in the complete product design process from concept to prototype? The program follows studio-based experiential learning covering user research, ideation, sketching, ergonomics, CAD modelling, material exploration, prototyping, usability testing, and final product development. Students work on real-world design briefs and industry-linked projects to complete the entire design lifecycle. What type of design studios and workshops are available? Students have access to well-equipped facilities including Prototyping Studio, Digital Rendering Studio, Ceramic & Clay Studio, Wood Studio, Paint Studio, Design Arcade, Fabrication Labs, and collaborative workspace studios for hands-on experimentation and product visualization. Which design software and technologies are taught? Training includes industry-standard tools such as SolidWorks, Rhino, Fusion 360, Figma, rendering software, and exposure to IoT integration, Arduino-based prototyping, AI-assisted design, wearable tech design, and emerging digital fabrication technologies. How are students connected with real industry projects and internships? The department integrates mandatory industrial training, live design projects, expert talks, workshops, industry collaborations, and internships with design studios, OEMs, startups, and manufacturing industries to ensure industry readiness. Is CUCET compulsory for admission? CUCET is not compulsory for admission. However, students may appear for CUCET to become eligible for up to 100% scholarship.
