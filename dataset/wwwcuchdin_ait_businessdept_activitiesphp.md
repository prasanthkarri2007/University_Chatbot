AIT Management, Chandigarh University organized One-of-its-kind, mega HR Event “HR Breakathon- Reimagine HR”. The HR Breakathon, held on 5th April 2023 was a resounding success, with more than 55 teams participating from across the university. The event challenged participants to reimagine HR and come up with the most unique and future-oriented solutions to the real life HR problems shared with them. It was a most unique event that enforced beyond the classroom learning in a collaborative way. The event concluded with presentations from the Top 21 teams and a panel of expert judges evaluating each submission and determining the winners. The quality of the submissions received was outstanding, with each team presenting a unique and innovative solution to the challenges facing HR today. The Chief Guest of the event was Mr. Navjot Singh Mighlani, Executive Director, Human Resources, Chandigarh University, and Guest of Honor were Mr. Nitin Sharma, Assistant Manager, HR at HonorVet, Fierfield, New Jersey, USA. Dr. Rupali Arora, Dr. Rishi Prakash Shukla, Dr. Muklesh Kumar Tiwari were part of the esteemed panel. The entire event was organized and conducted by Dr. Divya Juneja under the Leadership of Mr. Sanjeev Jain, Honorable Director, AIT Management and Mr. Gagan Vibhu, Respected Head of the Department, AIT Management-MBA. Overall, the HR Breakathon was a huge success, providing an opportunity for students to showcase their creativity, problem-solving skills, and deep understanding of the challenges facing HR professionals today. The winners of the event were awarded with Cash Prizes, Trophies and Certificates. We extend our warmest congratulations to all the teams who participated. We look forward to seeing how these solutions will shape the future of HR.
