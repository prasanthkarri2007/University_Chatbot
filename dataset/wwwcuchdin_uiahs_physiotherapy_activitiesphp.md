The Department of Physiotherapy at UIAHS successfully conducted the Red Cross Training Course from October 22nd to October 24th, 2024. The comprehensive first aid training covered essential topics such as Basic Life Support (BLS), management of wounds and fractures, handling burns and heat strokes, choking rescue techniques, managing shock and bleeding, and dealing with medical emergencies. The course focused on hands-on learning, with participants engaging in real-life scenarios and simulations to enhance their practical skills. The training not only prepared students for personal and professional emergency situations but also emphasized the importance of staying calm under pressure and making quick, effective decisions. The interactive nature of the session equipped students with life-saving skills, particularly vital for those pursuing healthcare professions such as physiotherapy. The course successfully met its objective of boosting participants’ confidence and competence in first aid, ensuring they are well-prepared to respond to emergencies both within and outside clinical settings. On September 12, 2023, the Department of Physiotherapy at the University Institute Of Allied Health Sciences hosted a General Health Assessment event to mark World Physiotherapy Day. With the participation of 29 student teams, supported by master's students, this event offered comprehensive health assessments to faculty members and academic dignitaries, including Prof (Dr.) RS Bawa and S. Satnam Singh Sandhu. Assessments encompassed critical health metrics like blood pressure, height, weight, hip-waist ratios, and BMI, accompanied by tailored physiotherapy guidance. A total of 402 assessments were conducted, and the event doubled as an educational platform, enlightening attendees about the pivotal role of physiotherapy in holistic health. In summary, the occasion showcased proactive health awareness and knowledge dissemination within Chandigarh University, all in honor of World Physiotherapy Day.
