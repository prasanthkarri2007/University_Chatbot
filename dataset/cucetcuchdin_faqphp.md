Hence, we have selected some frequently asked questions and answered them for you. Apply Today Frequently Asked Questions How can I register for CUCET? Please visit the website - https://cucet.cuchd.in to register your profile. Once you get registered you need to pay ₹1000/-. When can I apply for CUCET? There are two phases of CUCET in which you can apply anytime from Nov 2025 onwards However, the deadlines for different phases are available on the website- https://cucet.cuchd.in. Can I appear in multiple phases of CUCET for being eligible for higher percentage of scholarship? Yes, you can appear in multiple phases of CUCET (CUCET-I, CUCET-II). Two attempts per phase is allowed. You can appear in the next attempt/phase by paying ₹500/-. Scholarship slabs are different for different phases. By applying through off-line mode where do I have to send the CUCET application form? The application form purchased offline will have a unique number. You have to fill this number while registering at https://cucet.cuchd.in. You don’t have to pay ₹1000/- again if form is purchased offline. What should I do if I have not received the email/SMS post registration on CUCET? If you have not received any email/SMS after registration, you can email the details of your Application Number to cucet@cumail.in. If I have already scored 90% in 10+2 (Under Graduate) and seeking admission in Mandatory Programs. Do I have to appear in CUCET? If you have achieved more than 90% in your 10+2 (Under Graduate) examinations, you are exempted from taking the CUCET entrance test. However, if your 10+2 (Under Graduate) results have not yet been declared, it is recommended to appear for the CUCET as a precautionary measure. No, CUCET examination is conducted in online mode only. A student can appear using a laptop / PC with a camera. For more details visit https://cucet.cuchd.in Can I get an examination centre of my choice? No, you can appear in the test from your home only. Will I be issued any admit card for appearing in CUCET? The CUCET Test details will be sent on your CUCET registered Email ID. What is the syllabus of CUCET? The syllabus of CUCET is available on the website - https://cucet.cuchd.in/syllabus.php What will be the pattern of CUCET examination? There will be 100 multiple choice questions to be attempted in 120 minutes. For more information about the sample question papers, visit the website https://cucet.cuchd.in. Will there be any mock test available for CUCET examination? Yes, After successful registration student can appear for mock test, visit the website https://cucet.cuchd.in. What will be the medium of online exam of CUCET? The medium for the exam will be English only. Whereas for students with 10+2 Arts one can choose Hindi as a subject Is there any negative marking for CUCET? No, there will be no negative marking in CUCET. What will happen in case there is some problem in computer during CUCET online test? In such cases of computer system failure or any other related problems, Student need to contact technical helpline numbers and can reach out us at cucet@cumail.in. Moreover, the time lost will be duly counted and compensated as every second will be recorded by the examination server. If I appear in multiple phases of CUCET, which score will be considered? No. If one changes from Engineering to Non-Engineering, he needs to re-appear in CUCET again. Will the scholarship offered after clearing CUCET be continued in every semester? Scholarships Renewal Criteria & Renewal Process: Scholarships once given will be for the entire duration of the program but subject to mandatory meeting of set norms which will be reviewed every year. Once the set norms are met, the scholarship shall be renewed. Current Renewal Criteria CUCET Scholarship Recipients should apply for renewal every year. Performance: 7.5 CGPA at the end of each year. Student should not indulge in any activity of indiscipline. Student should not have E, F or I in any of the subject each year. Note: Scholarship shall be discontinued if there is any case of indiscipline against the student at any point of time. Who should I contact for details regarding CUCET? You can contact at CUCET helpline numbers 8146947000/8146948000 or Email at cucet@cumail.in. & Our Toll Free number is 1800-1212-88800 What is the procedure to renew the CUCET scholarship in next year? CUCET Scholarship once given will be for the entire duration of the program but subject to mandatory meeting of set norms which will be reviewed every year. Once the set norms are met, the scholarship shall be renewed. The current CUCET Scholarship renewal criteria is:- CUCET Scholarship Recipients should apply for renewal every year. Performance: 7.5 CGPA at the end of each year. Student should not indulge in any activity of indiscipline. Student should not have E, F or I in any of the subject each year. Is there any Timeline for CUCET related queries (result, test , technical issues etc. ) after appearing in the CUCET? Candidate will have to lodge an online request at cucet@cumail.in within 48 hrs of the appearing in the CUCET. What is the Timeline of declaring CUCET result? CUCET result will be declared post 24 hrs of appearing in the exam. Can I avail the CUCET scholarship after migration from other University? No, CUCET Scholarship option is not available in case of migration from other University. Can I avail CUCET scholarship, if I am an international student? No, CUCET Scholarship option is not available for International Students. In case, I change my program during admission/examination process , will it affect my CUCET scholarship? In case, candidate changes their course before or after the CUCET examination, the CUCET scholarship applicable shall be based on the final chosen or admitted course.
